About Dr. Syed

Your family doctor,
for the long run

Dr. Nihaad Syed believes a good consultation is one where you leave understanding what's going on and what to do about it. He takes the time to explain things clearly, and often sends patients home with resources and reading so they can stay informed between visits.

He studied medicine at the University of Sheffield and completed his medical training across London, England, working in paediatrics, obstetrics, psychiatry, emergency medicine, geriatrics, and rheumatology along the way. That range of experience means he is comfortable with the full breadth of family medicine, from newborn checks to complex elderly care.

He holds additional qualifications in child health and women's health, and taught medical students from Imperial College London. He brings that same instinct to patient care: clear explanations, useful resources to take home, and no jargon without context.

Outside the clinic, he reads widely, stays active, is never far from a camera, and is learning the guitar.

Dr. Syed is a UK-trained, Certified Family Physician licensed by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario and is currently welcoming new patients at the Whitby Clinic.

Services

Comprehensive care
for your whole family

From your child's first checkup to managing complex chronic conditions, Dr. Syed provides the full breadth of family medicine, with referrals to specialists when you need them.

Chronic Disease

Diabetes, high blood pressure, asthma, thyroid conditions. Ongoing care that keeps you on track, not just a prescription once a year.

Preventive Care

Cancer screening, blood pressure and cholesterol checks, routine health reviews, and immunisations for all ages. The things that are easier to deal with when they're caught early.

Mental Health

Anxiety, depression, stress, and sleep. If something's been weighing on you, bring it up. That's what the appointment is for.

Women's Health

Cervical screening, contraception, prenatal care, and menopause support from a doctor with additional training in women's health.

Men's Health

Prostate health, cardiovascular risk, and testosterone concerns. Thorough assessments and clear answers.

Care for Every Age

Well-baby visits and childhood immunisations through to medication reviews, fall prevention, and screening for memory concerns in older adults.

Everything Under One Roof

X-ray, ultrasound, and mammography (Ontario Breast Screening Program), LifeLabs blood work, a pharmacy, a registered dietitian, physiotherapy, massage therapy, orthotics and bracing, and a medical spa. All under the same roof at 200 Brock Street North, so more of your care stays in one place.
